[153] II. Wireline Telephone Subscriber Base
[154]  The number of wireline subscribers increased from 48.01 million at the end of
[155] July 2026 to 48.45 million at the end of August 2026. The net increase in the
[156] wireline subscriber base was 0.44 million with a monthly growth rate of
[157] 0.93%.
[158]  The overall wireline tele-density in India increased from 3.36% at the end of
[159] July 2026 to 3.39% at the end of August 2026. Urban and rural wireline tele-
[160] density were 8.34% and 0.58% respectively, at the end of August 2026.  The
[161] respective share of urban subscribers, and rural subscribers in total wireline
[162] subscribers were 89.08% and 10.92% respectively at the end of August 2026.
[163]  PSU access service providers viz. BSNL, MTNL, and APSFL together held
[164] 16.05% of the wireline market share at the end of August 2026. Detailed
[165] statistics of the wireline subscriber base are available at Annexure-I.

[226] III. Wireless Telephone (Mobile + FWA) Subscriber Base
[227] 
[228]       The number of wireless (mobile +
[229] Fixed Wireless Access) subscribers
[230] increased from 1306.27 million at the
[231] end of July 2026 to 1312.25 million at
[232] the end of August 2026, thereby
[233] registering a monthly growth rate of
[234] 0.46%. Total wireless subscription in
[235] urban areas increased from 753.86
[236] million in July 2026 to 759.11 million
[237] in August 2026. The subscription in
[238] rural areas also increased from 552.40
[239] million to 553.14 million during the
[240] same period. The monthly growth rate
[241] of urban and rural wireless
[242] subscriptions was 0.70% and 0.13%,
[243] respectively.
[244]   The wireless tele-density in India
[245] increased from 91.33% at the end of July
[246] 2026 to 91.69% at the end of August
[247] 2026. The urban wireless tele-density
[248] increased from 145.84% at the end of July
[249] 2026 to 146.66% at the end of August
[250] 2026. The rural wireless tele-density
[251] increased from 60.48% to 60.55% during
[252] the same period. The share of urban and
[253] rural wireless subscribers in the total
[254] number of wireless subscribers was
[255] 57.85% and 42.15% respectively, at the
[256] end of August 2026.
[257] 
[258] 
[259] Note: In the calculation of Tele-density figures, M2M cellular mobile connections have also been considered.
